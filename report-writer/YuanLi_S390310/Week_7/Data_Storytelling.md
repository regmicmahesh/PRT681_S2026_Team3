# Data Storytelling — Key Insight
**Yuan Li - S390310**

Looking at the two-week period (24 Aug – 4 Sep), Unauthorised Absence is noticeably weekday-skewed: Friday has the highest count (42), tapering down through Thursday (35), Wednesday (30), Tuesday (28), to Monday (25). Worth monitoring whether this pattern holds across future periods — it could suggest a tendency to extend weekends rather than random one-off absences.

Workforce Availability also dipped sharply around 28 August (down to roughly 52%) before recovering over the following days. Cross-referencing that date against the department breakdown shows the High School department carrying the largest share of both approved leave and unauthorised absence of any department — that's the first place a manager would look when investigating the dip.
