**Yuan Li - S390310**

## Excel
- What: spreadsheet application for organising and analysing data
- Why used: quick calculations, tables, simple reporting
- Who uses it: almost every business role, especially report writers/analysts
- Example: SUM/COUNTIF to summarise weekly job data

## Microsoft Word
- What: word processing tool
- Why used: writing formal reports and documentation
- Who uses it: report writers, business analysts
- Example: formatting a client service report with headings and tables

## GitHub
- What: version control platform
- Why used: tracking changes to files and collaborating with a team
- Who uses it: developers, and increasingly analysts/report writers for documentation
- Example: committing weekly research notes with a clear commit message
