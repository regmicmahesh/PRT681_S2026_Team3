# Methodology Notes - Week 3
**Yuan Li - S390310**

Reviewed Agile vs Waterfall approaches ahead of completing the Individual Project Canvas.

- Waterfall suits projects with low uncertainty and high repeatability (clear, fixed requirements).
- Agile suits projects with high novelty/change, where requirements evolve as the project progresses.
- For NT CleanTrack, an iterative/Agile-leaning approach was selected, since client and staff workflow requirements may need refining once the prototype is reviewed.
