Additional terms not already covered in the team's master glossary, written in plain, non-technical language.

## Tableau
Another tool (like Power BI) for turning data into charts and interactive dashboards, made by a different company (Salesforce). Many businesses use either Tableau or Power BI.

## Incremental Refresh (Power BI)
Instead of reloading ALL the data every time, this only updates the newest bit and leaves older data alone, making refreshes much faster for big reports.

## Data Dictionary
A simple list explaining what each column in a table means in plain words, so anyone new can understand the data without guessing.

## Conditional Formatting
Making numbers or cells automatically change color based on a rule, like turning a cell red if a job is overdue.

## Pivot / Unpivot (Power Query)
Pivot turns rows into columns; Unpivot does the opposite, turning columns back into rows.

## Report Subscription
Signing up to get a report automatically sent to you (e.g. by email) on a schedule, instead of remembering to check it yourself.
