# Report definition — NT Park Visits

The NT Park Visits report summarises estimated calendar-year visits for the parks in the Parks & Wildlife NT extract, with one Visits row per Park–Year and single-direction relationships from Year and Park to Visits; the baseline covers 22 parks across 2021–2025. Total Visits is SUM(Visits[Visits]) in the current filter context; Selected Year is MAX('Year'[Year]), so cleared year filters use the latest loaded year and multiple selections use the largest selected year; Selected Year Visits removes the Year-table filter and applies Selected Year, while retaining park and region filters; Previous Year Visits uses the same logic with Selected Year minus one; Visit Change subtracts Previous Year Visits from Selected Year Visits only when the previous-year value is not blank; and YoY Change uses DIVIDE(Selected Year Visits − Previous Year Visits, Previous Year Visits), returning blank when the denominator is blank or zero. Use Year[Year] and Park[Park]/Park[Region] for report filtering, because the time-comparison measures do not remove filters placed directly on fact-table year fields; the annual trend uses Total Visits and ignores the year slicer while retaining region filtering. Counts are rounded estimates of visits, not unique people, and the extract excludes the source's Other entries; 2021 has no prior-year baseline. The model imports the reviewed CSV from GitHub rather than scraping the government page; refreshing rereads that CSV and does not discover new government releases until a maintainer updates it. For the baseline, 2025/All should return 3,359,700 current visits, 3,189,600 previous visits, +170,100 Visit Change and +5.3% YoY. Source: Parks & Wildlife NT, https://dth.nt.gov.au/parks-and-wildlife/parks-and-wildlife-statistics-and-research/park-visitor-data (snapshot accessed 5 September 2026); all derived comparisons are student analysis.
