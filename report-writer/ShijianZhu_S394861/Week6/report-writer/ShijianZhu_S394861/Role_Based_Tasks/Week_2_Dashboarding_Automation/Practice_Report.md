## Purpose

This report compares visits to the NT parks and reserves listed in the government table. It uses a local topic with real data and keeps the dashboard small enough to check manually.

## Work prepared

The Excel workbook contains the annual source table, a long table, a report with year and region controls, a park comparison and source notes. The Power BI project recreates the main views and includes six DAX measures. The VBA module automates the conversion from 22 source rows to 110 park-year records.

## What the numbers show

Across the selected parks, estimated visits rose from 3,189,600 in 2024 to 3,359,700 in 2025: an increase of 170,100, or 5.3%. The highest total in the five-year extract was 3,584,600 in 2022, so the 2025 increase did not return the total to that level.

The pattern was different across regions. Darwin recorded 2,103,500 visits in 2025 compared with 1,990,400 in 2024. Katherine fell from 532,800 to 475,300, about 10.8%. This is why the report needs a regional view alongside the overall total.

At park level, Litchfield increased from 244,200 to 280,100, about 14.7%, while Elsey decreased from 201,600 to 146,800, about 27.2%. These are descriptive comparisons. The extract does not establish why these changes occurred.

Source: [Parks & Wildlife NT — Park visitor data](https://dth.nt.gov.au/parks-and-wildlife/parks-and-wildlife-statistics-and-research/park-visitor-data), accessed 5 September 2026. The calculations and comments above are this project's analysis. Counts are rounded estimates and are not unique visitor counts.
