# Suggested demonstration outline

This is a presentation aid, not an additional lecturer-specified requirement.

1. **Introduce the question (2 minutes).** Show the official NT source, the years covered and the distinction between visits and unique visitors.
2. **Show the Excel report (3 minutes).** Start with 2025/All, then select Darwin. Explain why the trend keeps all five years.
3. **Inspect the Power BI model (4 minutes).** Show Visits, Year and Park. Explain how the year change measures retain the region filter. Perform this part after Desktop validation.
4. **Compare the views (3 minutes).** Show the overall increase and Katherine's decrease. Avoid offering an unsupported cause.
5. **Run the macro (3 minutes).** In the tested XLSM copy, convert SourceWide into MacroOutput. Check 110 rows and rerun to show no duplicates.
6. **Discuss the limitations (2 minutes).** Annual estimates, selected parks, no 2020 baseline and no unique-visitor interpretation. Briefly explain the Tableau comparison plan and where Fabric could fit in a larger project.
